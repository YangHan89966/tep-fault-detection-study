# Third-party Tennessee Eastman data

This repository does not redistribute the four extended Tennessee Eastman RData files.

Official source:

Rieth, C. A., Amsel, B. D., Tran, R., and Cook, M. B. (2017), *Additional Tennessee Eastman Process Simulation Data for Anomaly Detection Evaluation*, Harvard Dataverse, https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/6C3JR1.

`data/DATA_MANIFEST.csv` records the exact filenames, byte sizes, and SHA-256 identities used in the study. Users must obtain the data from the official source and comply with its current terms. The 4.224 GB derived transport matrix and regenerated pickle caches are also omitted because they are large and reproducible from the listed sources.
