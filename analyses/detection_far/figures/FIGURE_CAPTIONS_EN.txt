Fig_1_Confirmation — Independent-stream confirmation. Thresholds from independent calibration runs. C1 compares signed and squared scores on the same selected mean-shift direction; C3 compares second-moment and mean-shift directions with a squared score. The Fault 9 comparison applies the same fixed historical monitor to controller-input and post-update records. Each point uses a separate limit for each arm and training replicate at a common ascending order-statistic rank of the calibration-run maxima. Each false-alarm rate is based on 400 normal runs, and each fault-specific rate on 200 fault runs. C1 and C3 are means over five frozen training replicates evaluated on the same trajectories; Fault 9 uses one frozen historical monitor. A clean detection requires no alarm before onset and a strict threshold crossing within the indicated interval after onset (40 h: top; 8 h: bottom). All fault runs remain in the denominator. The original limits (filled circles for the solid-line method; open squares for the dashed-line method) use rank 190 of 199 of the separate normal calibration maxima. Lines connect adjacent thresholds without smoothing or enforcing monotonicity. The display is restricted to 0–15% empirical false alarms; the data table includes the full range.

The Fault 9 comparison applies the same fixed historical monitor to controller-input and post-update records.
