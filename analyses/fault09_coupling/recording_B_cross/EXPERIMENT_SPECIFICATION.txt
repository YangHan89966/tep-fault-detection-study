TEP_P1_F9_RECORDING_B_CROSS_20261030_v1
Date: 2026-10-30 (Asia/Singapore)

Question
How much does the recording contrast change when B is estimated from eight
120-record blocks per normal design run instead of its first 120 records?

Scope
Post-update (native) and controller-input (snapshot), each crossed with the
original and batch-means B. Both second-moment and mean-shift pools use the
Signed two-sided CUSUM, k=0.05. Eight monitor outputs are required.
No new simulations, trajectories, recording types, block lengths or estimators.
This is a follow-up sensitivity analysis on an already analysed panel.

Data
Use the original paired 306 development runs: 40 normal design, 32 normal
anchor, 59 normal inner-calibration, 100 Fault-9 fit, 75 Fault-9 selection.
Use recording panel 2: 199 normal outer-calibration, 120 normal evaluation,
120 Fault-9 evaluation. Preserve case IDs, order and raw-file hashes.

Fixed processing
Use each recording's archived PCA and normal support (native 16 dimensions;
snapshot 15 of its original 17). Keep mean, scale, basis, support and whitening
fixed within recording. The cross-recording comparison includes these separately
learned pipelines. It is not a common-PCA intervention.

B construction
Let y have shape (40,960,d), after the original support and whitening.
Original: sample covariance (ddof=1) of the 40 first-block sums / sqrt(120).
Batch: reshape y to (40,8,120,d), sum within each block, divide by sqrt(120),
then sample covariance (ddof=1) across the resulting 320 block sums.
Blocks remain nested in 40 trajectories; 320 is not an independent run count.
Use the archived S51 implementation. Better conditioning alone is not evidence
of lower estimation error.

Learning and calibration
Rebuild both pools and all inherited metrics with only B changed. Retain the
original fit windows, normalization, ridge rules, selection tie-break, inner
calibration rank 57/59 and outer calibration rank 190/199. Use independent
calibration roles; do not tune on normal or fault evaluation data.
Record fitted-state and threshold hashes before scoring evaluation data.
First reproduce the original four monitors against their saved thresholds,
selection decisions and per-run alarm outcomes. Stop on a material mismatch.

Endpoints and comparisons
Report 40h and 8h clean detections, normal trajectory alarms, pre-fault alarms,
and mean penalized loss. Retain 2h as a secondary endpoint. Pre-fault alarms
and non-detections have 2400-minute loss; all runs stay in denominators.
Report absolute outputs and these differences:
1. controller-input minus post-update, within estimator and pool;
2. batch minus original, within recording and pool;
3. C2 = second-moment minus mean-shift, within recording and estimator;
4. interaction = recording contrast under batch minus that under original.
Compute 20,000 paired trajectory bootstrap draws with a fixed analysis seed;
reuse fault indices across every arm and use a separate shared normal index.
Report marginal percentile 95% intervals. These condition on the fitted
monitors and realized calibration, exclude learning variability, and do not
establish independence of the original simulator random streams.

Interpretation
An improvement under post-update alone does not establish a smaller recording
effect. Compare recording contrasts under both estimators and inspect 8h and
loss for 40h ceiling effects. A non-significant difference is not equivalence.
Report all eight outputs regardless of direction or magnitude.

Stored diagnostics
In a batch DESIGN.npz, raw_D remains the archived first-block covariance
diagnostic; B is the explicitly replaced batch estimate. raw_D is not the raw
covariance counterpart of the replaced B and is not used to reconstruct new
directions. B_ESTIMATOR_COMPARISON.npz identifies both B matrices explicitly.
