Graded measurement-noise coupling for the fixed historical Fault 9 monitor. Five shared-noise fractions retain 0, 3, 6, 9 or 12 of the controller measurement's twelve uniform noise components; the other components use an independent keyed stream. Each trajectory uses the same physical process and controller output across all fractions. The five fractions use the controller-input measurement time; actual post-update data are plotted separately as horizontal references, not as the rho=0 endpoint. (a) Pooled score variance over all 960 samples of 400 normal evaluation trajectories (sample variance, ddof=1). (b) Clean Fault 9 detection within 40 h and 8 h among 200 fault trajectories. (c) Any alarm over 48 h among 400 normal trajectories. Each arm has its own threshold, the 190th ascending maximum among the same 199 normal calibration trajectories. Error bars are marginal 95% Wilson intervals; connecting segments guide the eye and do not impose monotonicity. This is a post hoc sensitivity analysis of an already analyzed independent-stream panel, conditional on the fixed representation and realized calibration.
